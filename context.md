## Robot Specification
- Robot type: RC robot.
- Max footprint: 10cm x 10cm.
- Height: unlimited.
- Measurement state: starting position.
- Max mass: 500gr including batteries, excluding remote.
- Label required on robot front: each team member name + robot name.
- Label font/size free, must be clearly visible to referee.
- Banned: arena-damaging devices.
- Banned: devices that flip opponent by throwing.
- Banned: interference devices, e.g. signal jammer, strobe light, laser.
- Banned: arena adhesion systems, e.g. suction cup, diaphragm, sticky wheel.
- Banned: weapons, e.g. projectile, saw, flammable device.
- Banned: intentionally split robot into parts / separate robot parts.
- Banned: downforce devices, e.g. vacuum pump, magnet.

## General Match Mechanism
- Competition starts group stage.
- Each group: 4 teams.
- Qualified teams go knockout.
- Group allocation: random program during TM.
- Knockout pairing: random program after group stage.
- Random results final, cannot contest.
- During match: robot operated by 1 handler + 1 assistant.
- Handler + assistant must be registered team members in match.
- Only handler + assistant may approach arena.
- Handler may substitute only during interval between rounds.
- Non-competing teams may watch outside restricted area only.
- Non-competing teams must not interfere.
- Non-competing teams must turn off remote controllers to avoid signal interference.
- If robots lock together 10s OR both fall out same time: match paused, robots return to original start, match resumes until time expires.
- Before each match: 1min prep time in sterile area.
- Timeout: 1 per match, 45s, not allowed in group stage.
- Timeout request: after round ends, before next round starts, ask referee.
- Robot without timeout request must stay inside arena.
- During timeout: battery replacement/component changes NOT permitted.
- Referee/committee/judge decision final, cannot contest.

## Before Match
- Teams must understand call order from rundown.
- Team not yet competed cannot interfere with other teams, participants, robots.
- Other viewers stay outside competition arena.
- At start: no external assistance, no pushing, no other help.
- During competition: cannot replace power supply or components.
- Robot cannot split into multiple robots.
- Robot damage = participant responsibility.

## Group Stage
- Match: 3 rounds.
- Total match duration: 4min.
- Match ends when one team wins 2 of 3 rounds.
- Round ends when team scores point OR time expires.
- No timeout.
- Top 2 teams by total points proceed knockout.
- If tie by points, order decided by:
  1. Win time = time needed to earn points.
  2. Survive time = time robot stayed in arena before defeated/knocked out.

## Knockout Stage
- Redraw after each qualified round to determine next opponent.
- Match: 3 rounds.
- Total match duration: 5min.
- Match ends when one team wins 2 of 3 rounds.
- Round ends when team scores point OR time expires.
- Winner decided by total points.
- If total points equal: winner = fastest opponent-defeat time from previous 2 rounds.
- If neither team scores: both teams do not proceed.

## Scoring / Assessment
- +1 point: robot pushes opponent out arena.
- +1 point: opponent robot leaves arena, touches floor last.
- +1 point to opponent: robot unable move for 10s.
- Win time: time required to obtain point.
- Survive time: duration robot remains in arena before defeated/pushed out.
